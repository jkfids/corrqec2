\Fidel{This is an experimental section to explore connections between SPCs and hidden Markov models.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item An MPS with non-negative elements can be exactly represented as a hidden Markov model (HMM), where you treat the bond index as the hidden state. You can write the HMM transition and emission matrices in terms of the MPS tensors. This may be a way to leverage existing HMM theory to study SPCs.
    \item Side note: I've read a bit about entangled hidden Markov models (EHMMs), where it seems like there's a very straightforward correspondence with process tensors. An EHMMs is a quantum generalisation of HMMs, where the hidden system can be entangled with the observed system.
    \item Avenues to explore:
    \item Transfer matrix spectral gap and correlation length.
    \item Effective Markov order.
    \item There seems to be a body of literature of HMMs and model learning. Might be very useful for tomography? Decoding even? There's a connection between HMMs and belief propagation.
\end{enumerate}
